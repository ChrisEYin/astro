I've been dealing with something like a mutiny the last few weeks. I was ripping on people, I was raging, I was very tough on everyone. I made everyone meet with me on a regular basis in order to get what I needed out of them.

And I wasn't always the nicest. Sometimes people were quite frustrated with me and I could tell that it was hard for them to tolerate it.

But it's funny to then watch other people who are sick of what is going on and then try to do something about it resort to the same tactics to solve the problem. When they are stuck with the same situation and now they are responsible for it, they they do the same thing they complained about me about. And so that is the most ridiculous part of this whole thing.

Startups are not for fun. I think that the last few years of startup culture have made people think that everything is fun all the time, it's always up only, etc. Instead of people trying to figure out how to grow properly, how to think properly, etc.

I'm not going to get mad, it's just the realtiy of working with me. You will get feedback, you will have to grow with me, you will have to learn.

Accountability. For example Dan freaking out about AI and then inviting Zack to this thing without asking anyone for approval. And then freaking out when he is confronted with it.

That's a very toxic behavior. And I've let it go on too long.

I'm not good at delivering feedback. I can only do so in a way that cuts people to their core and is really bad. And then the other side of it is I am too shallow and light on it.

I need to find a middle ground where I can actually help these idiots figure out and take feedback from me without it being a thing. I think this week I'm going to focus on making sure that the team here understands what I'm looking for, what we are doing, and how we are doing things. And how these mistakes and these erros will not be tolerated going forward. Big and small. We will execute well and we will be execute as a team. If you cannot, it will not be tolerated.

Salman with the Fidelity stuff.
Dan with how he behaves in general and also the instant redemption feature.
Friel's speed and his control over the situation is a real problem.

And so ultimately these people will be in the fire and I'm going to let them know that this is what is expected.

I'm going to bring up this deck again -- your job is to make your org and other orgs better. Your tactical job is comms, it's this team first. My style is this -- I'm going to call things out instantly instead of letting them fester and waiting until our 1:1s.

And so this is what I should enforce on the team this week. I said it once already -- if you don't have something interesting to say once a week during SLT or to the team, then you will find yourself not on this team. Don't ask me for more things to do your existing job. Do your job and ask me for more to scale it up.

Trust is earned. You have to show me that you understand the basics of what we're doing here before we do anything further from this.
